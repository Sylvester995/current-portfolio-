About

I'm a junior full-stack developer and software engineering graduate of Rauf Denktaş University, where I paired an academic foundation with hands-on web development. What began as curiosity for HTML, CSS, and JavaScript grew into a real focus on frontend craft — building clean, user-friendly interfaces where design meets function.

Since then I've moved into full-stack development, shipping complete applications with AI-assisted workflows that let me build and iterate dramatically faster. I designed and deployed the software behind AfriCarbon, my charcoal business in Liberia — from a customer-facing storefront to a full business management system that tracks production, sales, and expenditure, projects financials, guides reinvestment, and generates downloadable financial reports. That gave me a builder's-eye view of the whole product: data, backend, interface, and deployment.

I care about turning concepts into polished, reliable products — and I'm looking for ambitious projects and collaborators to build the next one with.
